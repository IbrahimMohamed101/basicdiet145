## Landing page usage

Use only 3–5 screenshots in the App Showcase.

Prioritize screens that best prove:
- plan selection
- meal selection
- active subscription
- delivery/pickup management
- freeze/skip/manage controls

## Rule

Do not place all 10 screenshots on the landing page.

During Figma:
1. visually inspect the set at full size,
2. select 3–5 strongest/current screens,
3. avoid screens containing personal data,
4. use one primary screen and 2–4 supporting screens.
