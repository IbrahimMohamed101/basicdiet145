# A4 — Conversion Rules

**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Status:** ✅ COMPLETE

## Primary conversion

**First paid subscription through the Basic Diet app.**

The landing page should support this path:

`Landing page → app/store → subscription flow → checkout → first paid subscription`

## Primary CTA

**ابدأ اشتراكك**

Reason:
- direct,
- action-oriented,
- clear outcome,
- works in header, hero, plans, app section and final CTA.

## CTA hierarchy

### Primary
**ابدأ اشتراكك**

Use at:
- Header
- Hero
- App section
- Plans section
- Final CTA

### Secondary
Secondary links may exist below the fold, such as:
- شوف الوجبات
- شوف الباقات

They must never visually compete with the primary CTA above the fold.

### WhatsApp
Can exist in footer/contact or as a lower-priority support channel.

Do not make WhatsApp the primary conversion path for the evergreen landing page.

---

## Message matching

For paid social traffic:
- the ad promise should match the hero promise,
- the food visual style should match the campaign creative,
- campaign-specific landing variants may change the headline while keeping the same page structure.

Example:
If an ad leads with meal variety, the hero should not suddenly lead with discount.

---

## Above-the-fold rules

The first screen must contain:
1. clear food visual,
2. one headline,
3. one short supporting statement,
4. one primary CTA,
5. one compact proof cue.

Do not place:
- a pricing table,
- multiple offer cards,
- a large WhatsApp button,
- multiple equal CTAs,
- long paragraphs.

---

## Proof rule

Every major claim should be paired with evidence.

### Taste
Proof:
- real meal visuals,
- approved taste review.

### Flexibility
Proof:
- 100g / 150g / 200g,
- 1–5 meals/day,
- app screenshots,
- delivery/pickup.

### App control
Proof:
- real screenshots,
- verified public app capabilities.

### Quality/trust
Proof:
- verified rating signal,
- approved reviews,
- preparation/packaging visuals where strong.

---

## Risk reduction

We will not invent a guarantee or "cancel anytime" claim.

Instead reduce risk through:
- clear plan durations,
- visible configuration choices,
- real app control,
- real proof,
- clear FAQ.

---

## Pricing rule

Price is not the hero story.

Plans section may show:
- plan duration,
- flexibility,
- a verified starting price if approved later.

Discount codes should not dominate the evergreen page.

---

## Mobile conversion rules

- CTA visible without excessive scrolling.
- Video must not delay interaction.
- Sticky CTA may be tested later only if it does not obstruct content.
- Tap targets must be large and clear.
- No autoplay audio.
- App/store routing must be platform-aware when implemented.

---

## Measurement rules

Track:
- hero CTA click
- header CTA click
- app-section CTA click
- plans CTA click
- final CTA click
- platform/store outbound click
- scroll reach to food/app/plans/proof sections
- checkout start where attribution is technically possible
- first paid subscription where attribution is technically possible
