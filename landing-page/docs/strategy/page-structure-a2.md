# A2 — Page Structure

**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Method:** `elayadesign/ai-design-skills / landing-page-design`  
**Status:** ✅ COMPLETE  
**Selected layout:** **A — Classic hero + sections**

## Why Layout A

Basic Diet can be understood quickly from:
- premium food video,
- one clear value proposition,
- simple subscription explanation,
- app proof.

The page still needs a structured argument to overcome:
1. taste / boredom,
2. control / flexibility,
3. quality / trust.

A long-form story would add unnecessary friction for social-first mobile traffic.  
A minimal conversion page would not provide enough proof.  
A comparison page is not the main evergreen use case.

---

# Page argument

The page should move the visitor through:

**Desire → Relevance → Proof → Control → Simplicity → Value → Trust → Action**

In customer language:

1. **ده شكله أكل حلو فعلًا**
2. **ومش لازم يكون الدايت ممل**
3. **وعندي اختيارات تناسب يومي**
4. **والتطبيق مخليني متحكم**
5. **والاشتراك مفهوم وبسيط**
6. **وفي ناس فعلًا جربته ورضيت عنه**
7. **تمام، أبدأ من التطبيق**

---

# Final section order

## 0. Header

### Purpose
Navigation only. Do not compete with conversion.

### Contains
- Basic Diet logo
- short navigation anchors only
- one primary CTA

### Recommended anchors
- الوجبات
- كيف يعمل
- الباقات
- التطبيق
- الأسئلة

### Mobile
Compact navigation. No large menu tree.

---

## 1. Hero — Appetite first

### Job
Answer immediately:
- what Basic Diet is,
- why it is desirable,
- what the visitor should do next.

### Main visual
Premium short food video.

### Content hierarchy
1. Headline
2. short supporting copy
3. primary CTA
4. compact proof cue
5. optional store/platform cue

### Rule
There is **one primary CTA above the fold**.

### Objection handled
**Taste / boredom**

---

## 2. Value / proof strip

### Job
Give fast factual reasons to keep scrolling.

### Candidate facts
- 100g / 150g / 200g
- 1–5 meals per day
- delivery or pickup
- manage the subscription from the app

### Objection handled
**Control / relevance**

---

## 3. Food experience

### Job
Prove taste and variety visually.

### Content
Curated real-food gallery, not the full menu.

Recommended:
- 4–8 selected dishes
- visually different categories
- meal name
- one useful verified fact per card when available

### Argument
Show familiar desirable food first, then measured nutrition.

### Objection handled
**Taste / boredom**

### Proof
A short approved taste-review cue can sit here.

---

## 4. Tagline reveal — Belief shift

### Job
Create a memorable mid-page statement.

### Directional copy
**الأكل المحسوب مش لازم يكون ممل.  
اختيارات تحبها، بكميات تناسب روتينك.**

Not final copy.

---

## 5. Why Basic Diet

### Four benefit territories

#### أكل تستمتع به
Variety and familiar meals reduce diet fatigue.

#### الكمية على حسب روتينك
Different portion and meal-count options give control.

#### قرار أقل كل يوم
Subscription reduces planning/preparation friction.

#### كل شيء من التطبيق
The user can manage the subscription experience digitally.

### Rule
Each benefit should later have concrete proof.

---

## 6. How it works

### Three steps

1. **اختر الباقة**
2. **اختر وجباتك**
3. **تابع اشتراكك**

### Objection handled
**"الاشتراك معقد؟"**

---

## 7. App control — Product proof

### Job
Prove flexibility is real.

### Prioritized screenshots
1. plan/subscription selection
2. meal selection
3. subscription management
4. delivery/pickup management
5. useful status screen

### Publicly supported capabilities
- browsing plans
- subscribing
- managing delivery
- freezing
- skipping days
- pickup

### CTA
Primary CTA can repeat here.

---

## 8. Plans

### Job
Help the visitor understand commitment levels without overwhelming them.

### Structure
- 7 days
- 26 days
- 30 days

### Show
- duration
- simple fit/use case
- configuration flexibility
- verified starting price only if later approved

### Do not show
A giant `days × grams × meals × add-ons` matrix.

### CTA
Configure/start in app.

---

## 9. Quality / customer proof

### Job
Resolve trust before FAQ/final CTA.

### Potential proof
- verified rating signal
- approved review excerpts
- cleanliness/quality language
- preparation/packaging visuals

### Structure
Prefer:
- one strong rating/stat cue,
- 2–4 short real reviews,
- real proof visuals.

---

## 10. FAQ

### V1 themes
1. كيف أختار الباقة المناسبة؟
2. كم عدد الوجبات التي أقدر أختارها يوميًا؟
3. هل أقدر أختار وزن الوجبة؟
4. كيف أختار أو أغير وجباتي؟
5. هل يوجد توصيل واستلام؟
6. كيف أدير اشتراكي من التطبيق؟
7. هل توجد معلومات السعرات أو الماكروز؟
8. أين تتوفر الخدمة؟

Cancellation is intentionally excluded.

---

## 11. Final CTA

### Job
Give one decisive next step.

### Rule
Same primary action as the hero. No new competing offer.

---

## 12. Footer

### Required
- logo
- basic navigation
- contact/social
- privacy/terms/policies required for launch
- business/copyright info as appropriate
