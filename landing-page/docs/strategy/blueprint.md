**Status:** PRE-CODE LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-10-07

## Layout

**A — Classic hero + sections**

## Conversion path

`Food desire → relevance → proof → control → simplicity → value → trust → action`

## Section order

0. Header
1. Hero video
2. Value/proof strip
3. Food experience
4. Tagline reveal
5. Why Basic Diet
6. How it works
7. App experience
8. Plans
9. Quality/customer proof
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA
12. Footer

## Structural rules

- Food desire before pricing.
- One primary CTA above the fold.
- Proof sits next to claims.
- App is a major proof section.
- Plans stay simple: 7 / 26 / 30 days.
- No huge pricing/configuration matrix.
- Trust proof comes before FAQ.
- Final CTA repeats hero action.
- Mobile preserves the argument order.
