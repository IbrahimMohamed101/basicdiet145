## First 7 recommended publishing directions

Do not treat these as a rigid calendar. They are a balanced starting sequence:

1. C003 — challenge the boring-diet stereotype.
2. C006 — explain portion flexibility.
3. C016 — kitchen/packaging trust.
4. C001 — familiar-food desire (kabsa).
5. C011 — convenience/lifestyle problem.
6. C020 — subscription flexibility.
7. C019 — trial-to-subscription conversion angle.

## Repetition guardrail

Do not publish:
- more than 2 direct promo/discount posts in a 7-post sequence,
- the same dish angle twice within a short window,
- nutrition numbers that are not verified from the live source,
- customer-review language as a testimonial with identity/name unless approved.

## Performance fields to add after publishing

For every published item capture:
- reach/views,
- watch time when available,
- saves/shares/comments,
- profile/landing visits,
- registrations,
- checkout starts/users,
- first paid subscriptions when attributable,
- revenue when attributable,
- learning.
