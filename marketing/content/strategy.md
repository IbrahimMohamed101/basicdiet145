# Content Strategy

**Version:** v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-07

## Business objective

Content exists to create and convert demand into first paid subscriptions, then support retention/repeat purchase.

## Starting content pillars

| Pillar | Starting weight | Primary purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Food Desire | 30% | Make the product visually/descriptively desirable |
| Education | 20% | Build understanding/trust using verified product/nutrition facts |
| Lifestyle / Problem | 20% | Connect product to real daily problems and routines |
| Trust / Proof | 15% | Reduce risk through real proof and operational credibility |
| Conversion / Offer | 15% | Ask for the subscription/checkout action |

Weights are hypotheses and must be updated from performance.

## Core creative system

Every content concept should document:
- audience/segment,
- funnel stage,
- business objective,
- content pillar,
- source insight,
- source asset,
- hook,
- format,
- CTA,
- KPI,
- result/learning after measurement.

## Grounding requirement

A concept should trace to at least one real source:
- menu/product fact,
- real asset,
- customer insight,
- prior winning content,
- current offer,
- real business/funnel data.

No invented testimonials or unsupported claims.

## Daily content decision protocol

When asked "ننزل إيه النهارده؟":

1. Check current state/weekly objective.
2. Check recent content to prevent accidental repetition.
3. Check whether the funnel currently needs awareness, trust, conversion, or retention support.
4. Check active offer and whether direct selling is appropriate.
5. Select the strongest available asset/angle.
6. Define one primary KPI.
7. Produce:
   - objective,
   - format,
   - hook,
   - script/design direction,
   - caption,
   - CTA,
   - supporting stories,
   - asset reference,
   - paid-promotion suitability.
8. After publishing, append it to `content-log.md`.
9. After the measurement window, add results and learning.

## Brand rule

Do not make discounts the default reason to buy. Build:
**taste + choice + convenience**, supported by verified proof.
