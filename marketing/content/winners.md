# Content & Creative Winners

Only place a creative/angle here after evidence supports it.

## Qualification guidance

A "winner" should be tied to the objective it was meant to achieve.

Examples:
- awareness winner: unusually strong qualified reach/watch behavior,
- consideration winner: strong profile/landing/checkout intent,
- conversion winner: efficient paid subscribers/revenue,
- retention winner: measurable repeat/reactivation effect.

Do not label a post a business winner based on views alone.
