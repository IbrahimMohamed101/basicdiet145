# Frameworks Adapted for Basic Diet

This Marketing OS adapts selected concepts from the open-source `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` repository and general performance-marketing practice.

Reference:
- https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills
- Repository license at time of review: MIT.

We do **not** adopt every skill. Basic Diet is a local consumer food/subscription business, so SaaS/B2B-specific workflows are excluded unless later relevant.

## Adopted concepts

### Product Marketing Context
Maintain one durable source for product, customer, positioning, proof, goals, and messaging before generating tactics.

### Customer Research / VOC
Prioritize real customer language, pains, desired outcomes, objections, triggers, alternatives, and churn reasons. Label insight confidence instead of treating one anecdote as universal truth.

### Competitor Intelligence
Separate:
- observed facts,
- inferred interpretation,
- business implication.

Never claim an unobserved competitor feature/weakness as fact.

### Grounded Creative
Creative should trace back to real inputs:
- proven content/ads,
- customer reviews,
- comments/objections,
- menu/product facts,
- brand assets,
- measured performance.

Never invent claims, testimonials, or metrics.

### Analytics
Track data because it changes a decision. Do not collect vanity events without an intended action.

### Attribution
Attribution is a decision model, not perfect causal truth. Keep source/campaign conventions consistent and reconcile platform claims against actual paid revenue.

### Marketing Loops
Use recurring learning loops:
`Measure -> Diagnose -> Hypothesize -> Execute -> Verify -> Document -> Repeat`.

Strategy and spend changes require human review unless explicitly authorized with safeguards.

## Basic Diet-specific additions

- Restaurant/menu intelligence.
- Content memory tied to meals/assets.
- First-paid-subscription North Star.
- Local consumer funnel and retention focus.
- Daily social execution protocol.
