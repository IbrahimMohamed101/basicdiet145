# Paid conversion projection — 2026-11-24

Draft implementation, no live changes. Separate admin-only read endpoint under landing analytics: GET /paid-conversions?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD. One row per paid, applied, positive SAR payment with a matching completed checkout, user and subscription. No client-side purchase claims or new payment writes. Counts completion date in Riyadh; excludes refunded and incomplete payments. First recorded purchase across channels and renewal payment type are separate dimensions. Lead matching uses only consented leads, unique account phone and last eligible lead within 30 days before payment; no direct attribution for store-only installs. No PII is returned. Tests use synthetic data. Require isolated integration test and dashboard display before release. No live baseline or causal attribution claimed.
