# Positioning & Messaging

**Version:** v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-07

## Working positioning

> **Basic Diet = أكل حقيقي تحبه، بكميات محسوبة، بخيارات كثيرة، ويوفر عليك قرار الأكل كل يوم.**

## Messaging hierarchy

### 1. Taste — الطعم
Healthy eating should not feel like punishment or repetitive "diet food."

### 2. Choice — الاختيار
Communicate the customer's ability to choose meal count, portion size, menu options, and fulfillment.

### 3. Convenience — الراحة
Reduce daily food planning and preparation decisions.

### Supporting proof
- calories/macros when verified,
- menu ingredients,
- real meal photography/video,
- real customer proof,
- preparation/packaging/delivery proof,
- real subscription/product facts.

## Content language

Prefer:
- direct Saudi/Arabic consumer language,
- appetizing product-first hooks,
- specific facts over generic wellness slogans,
- real customer wording when available.

Avoid:
- generic "ابدأ حياة صحية الآن" as the only value proposition,
- invented transformation claims,
- guaranteed weight-loss claims,
- overusing promotional urgency,
- AI-sounding generic copy.

## Example angle

"مين قال الدايت مفيهوش كبسة؟"

This is an example creative direction, not a permanently approved slogan. Its performance should be measured before scaling the pattern.
