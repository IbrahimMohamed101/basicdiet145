## Initial audience hypotheses

These remain testing segments.

### Busy professional
Problem hypothesis: lacks time/energy to prepare and plan measured meals.
Message hypothesis: "وجبتك محسوبة وجاهزة بدل دوشة كل يوم."

### Fitness / gym customer
Problem hypothesis: wants clear portions/protein and repeatable routine.
Message hypothesis: choose portion and meal count around your routine.

### Weight-management customer
Problem hypothesis: gets bored with restrictive/repetitive diet meals.
Message hypothesis: familiar, varied food can fit a measured routine.

### General healthy-lifestyle customer
Problem hypothesis: wants convenience without an extreme diet identity.
Message hypothesis: make measured food a normal part of the day.

### Former customer
Problem hypothesis: routine was interrupted.
Message hypothesis: make returning to a structured routine easy.

## Early customer-language signals

### Signal A — "healthy without sacrifice"
Public review language supports an emotional job around eating enjoyable/crispy food while feeling that the choice still fits a healthier routine.

**Confidence:** Medium-low  
Reason: appears clearly in public review language, but sample size is small.

### Signal B — taste + cleanliness
Public reviews explicitly praise taste and cleanliness/quality.

**Confidence:** Medium-low  
Reason: repeated across more than one public review, still a small public sample.

### Signal C — trial can trigger subscription
One public reviewer described trying the meals and then joining a membership/subscription.

**Confidence:** Low but commercially important  
Reason: single observed public case. Treat as a hypothesis for a "try -> subscribe" conversion path.

## Extraction fields

For every meaningful source, extract anonymously:
- pain/problem,
- desired outcome,
- trigger event,
- objection,
- alternative considered,
- buying reason,
- churn/cancellation reason,
- exact customer phrase,
- segment/context.

## Confidence labels

- **High:** repeated independently across 3+ sources and consistent behavior/evidence.
- **Medium:** repeated in 2 sources or limited to one segment.
- **Low:** single anecdote or unverified interpretation.

Never store customer PII in this repository.
