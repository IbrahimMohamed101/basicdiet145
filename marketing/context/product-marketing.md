# Product Marketing Context — Basic Diet

**Document version:** v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-07

## Product overview

**Brand:** Basic Diet  
**Business:** Healthy meal restaurant / meal subscription business in Saudi Arabia, focused operationally on Jeddah.

**Core offer:** Customers choose a subscription duration, portion weight, meals per day, fulfillment option, and optional add-ons.

Known subscription dimensions:
- Duration: 7 / 26 / 30 days
- Portion: 100g / 150g / 200g
- Meals/day: 1–5
- Fulfillment: delivery or pickup

The wider menu/asset library includes familiar meals, breakfast, sandwiches, salads, and desserts. Available product data includes calories/macros for many items.

## Jobs to be done — current hypotheses

Customers may hire Basic Diet to:
1. Avoid deciding/cooking healthy meals every day.
2. Control portions/calories/macros while still eating enjoyable food.
3. Maintain a diet/fitness routine with less effort.
4. Access familiar food without the stereotypical repetitive "diet meal" experience.

These remain hypotheses until supported by VOC and behavioral data.

## Primary commercial conversion

**First paid subscription**

Secondary:
- repeat/renewed subscription,
- higher-quality AOV/revenue,
- retention,
- referral.

## Differentiation hypothesis

Basic Diet can potentially differentiate through:
- familiar and enjoyable food,
- visible portion choice,
- menu variety,
- measurable nutritional information,
- convenience of subscription.

Do not turn this hypothesis into an unsupported market claim. Validate through customer language and performance.

## Anti-goals

Marketing should not:
- become a discount-only feed,
- make unsupported weight-loss/medical promises,
- optimize primarily for followers/views,
- use invented nutrition or performance claims.
