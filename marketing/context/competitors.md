# Competitor & Market Context — Pass 01

**Research date:** 2026-10-07  
**Scope:** Jeddah/Saudi healthy-meal and meal-subscription market.  
**Rule:** Observed facts are separated from implications. Pricing/promos are time-sensitive and must be rechecked before use.

## 1. Calo — Saudi market benchmark

Source:
- https://calo.app/ar-sa/plans-and-packages

### Observed
- Positions around personalized meal-plan design.
- Offers balanced, custom-macro and low-carb paths.
- Lets users choose meals/day and days/week.
- Uses weekly/monthly and longer payment cycles.
- Public page shows starting pricing around 50 SAR/day.
- Promotes gym-membership integrations and BNPL options.

### Implication for Basic Diet
Calo owns a strong personalization/technology/convenience territory. Basic Diet should not imitate the same message generically; it should make its own flexibility tangible through grams, meals/day, familiar menu and local food desire.

## 2. Diet Center KSA — clinical/trust benchmark

Sources:
- https://www.dietcenter.com.sa/ar/programs/find-your-balance-full/
- https://www.dietcenter.com.sa/ar/programs/
- public app-store listings

### Observed
- Strong nutrition/clinic authority.
- Balanced-program messaging with calorie-controlled daily structures.
- Large recipe/menu variety.
- Delivery and branch pickup.
- Multiple duration choices.
- Dietitian/clinic services and health-program framing.

### Implication for Basic Diet
Diet Center has more authority to compete on clinical nutrition. Basic Diet should use verified nutrition facts without pretending to be a clinic; lead with taste, choice and convenience, then add nutrition proof.

---

## 3. Diet King — direct/local Jeddah competitor

Sources:
- https://dietking.sa/clientboard/package/20
- local business listings
- public reviews

### Observed
- Multiple goal/package structures.
- Trial-package concept is explicitly present.
- Subscription terms emphasize advance meal selection, pause rules and branch/pickup flexibility.
- Public local branches have significant review volume.
- Public competitor reviews show both strong taste/freshness praise and occasional complaints about dryness/limited availability.

### Implication for Basic Diet
Two useful opportunities:
1. make variety/availability visible before customers worry about repetition,
2. use real meal-quality/taste proof rather than only package tables.

---

## 4. OctaFood — aggressive offer/value benchmark

Sources:
- https://octafoodksa.com/
- https://octafoodksa.com/en/home/

### Observed
- Strong goal-based segmentation: weight loss, gym, bulking and special-use packages.
- Highlights 120+ options.
- Promotes free delivery and nutritionist consultation.
- Uses strong crossed-out promotional pricing.
- Public page shows 24-day plans with explicit protein/carb quantities.

### Implication for Basic Diet
The category is already crowded with "goal + grams + discount + free delivery" messaging. Basic Diet needs stronger creative differentiation than another package-price grid.

---

## 5. Smart Diet / local healthy restaurants — local category signal

Sources:
- local/public Jeddah listings and review aggregators.

### Observed
Public review language for local healthy restaurants frequently values:
- taste,
- reasonable price,
- cleanliness,
- variety,
- gym/bulking/cutting fit.

Complaints often center on:
- lack of variety,
- inconsistent preparation,
- limited sauces/options.

### Implication for Basic Diet
Content should repeatedly prove:
- variety,
- real taste,
- menu rotation/choice,
- consistent quality,
- portion flexibility.

## Category pattern

The market repeatedly sells:
- healthy,
- calories/macros,
- weight-loss/gym goals,
- convenience,
- personalization,
- dietitian authority,
- discounts/free delivery.
